# 宠物与交互参考

- Behavior, capsule layout and typography reference: https://github.com/Wunsch2048/codex-bailong-desktop-pet . Original white-dragon assets: https://github.com/jingh12312-rgb/bailong-desktop-pet . No code, white-dragon artwork or Python runtime from either project is redistributed. The optional loader reads original local materials or fetches the author's pinned release directly, verifies its size/hash, and converts locally. The original ASSETS/NOTICE do not grant a general redistribution license.
- deepseek酱 by fightingshine, Boba by railly, Tiko by huigegood, Wangcai by boxu-openai: https://petdex.dev/zh . Codie: https://codexpet.xyz/pets/codie/ . Downloaded packs contained pet.json and spritesheets but no separate asset license. Their original rights remain with the corresponding creators. These assets are not relicensed under this repository's MIT code license.
- 奥黛塔 / Odette by Zlllo; 爱弥斯 / Aemeath and 穗穗 / Suisui by mizunagare; 流萤·春日手信 by YaKun9: https://github.com/YaKun9/codex-pets . Each original LICENSE.md is retained beside its atlas; see also YaKun-ASSETS-LICENSE.md. These notices permit non-commercial use and sharing subject to their stated conditions and underlying character rights. They prohibit commercial/paid distribution and sublicensing.
- 小蕾米 / Remielle by HanaAyane / 小蕾米: https://github.com/HanaAyane/remielle-codex-pet . No artwork is redistributed here. The loader fetches the pinned original atlas and its ASSET-USAGE.md / NOTICE.md directly from the author for local use. The author describes AI-assisted redrawing and manual editing of source material associated with Zenless Zone Zero. Those notices provide limited non-commercial fan-use terms, not MIT or a general license to underlying character artwork. Both original notices remain with the local imported copy.
- Community resource directory reference: https://github.com/T-Zevin/Awesome-Codex-Pets . Linked projects and artworks retain their own terms; this navigation repository is not treated as an asset bundle.
- Sprites were losslessly converted from WebP to PNG to run offline in the native Windows renderer; character art and animation frames were not redrawn. Exact source URLs, creators, revision and downloaded file checksums are in source/assets/pets/sources.json and individual source.json files.
- Interaction behavior, physics, quota parsing, transparent-window rendering, and the compact/expanded UI in this branch are independently implemented. The ring uses vector paths and the bundled Chakra Petch font (SIL OFL, see source/assets/fonts/ChakraPetch-OFL.txt).
- The Hub logo was supplied by the project user, and is kept unchanged at source/assets/brand/codexhub.png. The refresh icon is the original MIT Phosphor arrow-clockwise SVG, rasterized for the native renderer; the SVG and Phosphor-LICENSE are retained in source/assets. Historical mountain icon resources remain for compatibility.
- Collision sparks are procedural vector effects. The built-in two-note interaction sound is synthesized locally by PetSound.Create; no voice assets from these sites are included.
